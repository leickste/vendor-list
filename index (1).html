Selling

Thinking about selling?

Here's how it works with me.

  1. Send me your address

    I'll pull recent sales and current competition in your neighborhood.

  2. We walk the house together

    I'll point out what's worth fixing, what to leave alone, and who can help.

  3. Price it right and list it

    Professional photos, MLS, and marketing to get the right buyers in the door.

  4. Negotiate through closing

    Offers, inspection, appraisal, and title. I handle the back-and-forth.

Buying

Ready to buy?

Five steps from first showing to keys in hand.

  1. Get pre-approved

    Know your budget before you fall in love with a house. See my lenders →

  2. Search

    I'll set up a live search so you see new listings the minute they hit.

  3. Tour and write offers

    We'll compare homes, and I'll write a strong offer when you find the one.

  4. Inspect and insure

    Inspection, insurance quotes, and appraisal. I'll line up people I trust.

  5. Close

    Final walkthrough, sign at title, get your keys.
